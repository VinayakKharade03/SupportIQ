Bluetooth Connection Troubleshooting

If your earbuds or headphones won't connect via Bluetooth, try these steps in order:

1. Ensure the earbuds are charged. Low battery can prevent pairing.
2. Turn off Bluetooth on your phone, wait 10 seconds, then turn it back on.
3. Forget the device in your phone's Bluetooth settings, then re-pair from scratch.
4. Reset the earbuds: place both earbuds in the case, open the lid, and press
   and hold the button inside the case for 10 seconds until the LED flashes
   red and white alternately. This indicates a factory reset.
5. Make sure you are within 1 meter of the device during initial pairing.
6. Check that no other device is currently connected to the earbuds - most
   models only support one active connection at a time.

If none of these steps work, the earbuds may have a hardware fault. In that
case, direct the customer to raise a replacement or repair request.
