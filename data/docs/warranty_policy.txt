Warranty Policy

All boAt audio products come with a 1-year manufacturer warranty from the
date of purchase, covering manufacturing defects in materials and workmanship.

What is covered:
- Battery not holding charge under normal use
- Audio drivers producing distorted or no sound
- Charging case not charging the earbuds
- Physical button or touch controls not responding

What is NOT covered:
- Physical damage from drops, water damage beyond IPX rating, or misuse
- Cosmetic wear and tear
- Loss or theft of the product
- Damage caused by unauthorized repair attempts

To claim warranty, the customer needs their order ID or proof of purchase
date. Replacement or repair is decided after inspection; a rough turnaround
is 7-10 business days from when the product is received.
