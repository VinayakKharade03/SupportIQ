Charging Case Issues

If the charging case is not charging the earbuds:

1. Check the case's own battery level (usually shown by an LED indicator).
   If the case itself is dead, charge it fully via USB-C for at least 1 hour.
2. Clean the charging pins inside the case with a dry cloth - dust or lint
   buildup is a very common cause of charging failure.
3. Make sure the earbuds are seated properly in the case; you should feel
   a slight magnetic click and see the LED light up.
4. Try a different charging cable and power source, since a faulty cable is
   a common culprit.
5. If only one earbud charges, the issue is likely with that specific
   earbud's contact pins rather than the case.

If the case LED does not light up at all even when plugged in, the case
itself may be faulty and should be flagged for replacement.
